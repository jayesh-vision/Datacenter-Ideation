Open any screen directly — with or without the app's menu

Every screen of the tool has its own URL. Add shell=false to show the screen alone, without the left menu and the top header, ready for an iframe in your platform. Pick the persona and the geography in the URL too. The controls above rewrite every link and snippet on this page.

The two parameters

ParameterValuesEffect
shelltrue · falsefalse hides the left menu and the top header. true shows them. Without it the app shows them, unless the same browser tab or iframe already chose — the choice sticks while the user clicks around inside the frame.
personanetwork · colocation · cloud · enterprise · neocloudWhich business model the whole estate is shown as. Remembered by the browser. The persona switch lives in the header, so when shell=false set it here.
geoin · usWhere the estate is: India (24 sites across 20 circles, ₹ with Cr/L, dates 21-Sep-2026) or the USA (24 sites across 20 states, $ with K/M, dates Sep 21, 2026). Same equipment, alarms and story; different sites, customers, people and currency. Remembered by the browser.
Combine with any screen's own parameters, in any order: /incidents?view=sev1&shell=false&persona=cloud.

Override from Vercel

When a link you don't control asks for one model, the deployment can change it. Set these in Vercel → Project → Settings → Environment Variables, then redeploy. They win over the URL and the user's saved choice.

VariableRuleEffect
VITE_PERSONA_OVERRIDEus:network=colocationUSA + Network requests open as Colocation; everything else unchanged.
network=colocationNetwork becomes Colocation in both geographies.
colocationAlways Colocation, whatever is asked.
VITE_GEO_OVERRIDEus · in=usAlways the USA · India requests become the USA.
Several rules: separate with commas, the first match wins. Delete the variable and redeploy to remove the override.
