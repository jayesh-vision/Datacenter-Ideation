Open any screen directly — with or without the app's menu

Every screen of the tool has its own URL. Add shell=false to show the screen alone, without the left menu and the top header, ready for an iframe in your platform. The controls above rewrite every link and snippet on this page.

The two parameters

ParameterValuesEffect
shelltrue · falsefalse hides the left menu and the top header. true shows them. Without it the app shows them, unless the same browser tab or iframe already chose — the choice sticks while the user clicks around inside the frame.
personanetwork · colocation · cloud · enterpriseWhich business model the whole estate is shown as. Remembered by the browser. The persona switch lives in the header, so when shell=false set it here.
Combine with any screen's own parameters, in any order: /incidents?view=sev1&shell=false&persona=cloud.

Embed in your platform


      

      
Without the header there is no search box or alarm bell. Each screen keeps its own Ask Opstral AI button, and ⌘K still opens search. Links inside the screen stay inside the iframe and keep the same mode.
