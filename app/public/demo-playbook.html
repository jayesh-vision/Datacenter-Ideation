The story

"The 13:20 call" — one afternoon in a datacenter operations team

It is Thursday, 13:20. A utility feed has tripped at the Delhi datacenter, a backhaul is flapping in Pune and a cooling unit is down in Nagpur. The CEO's office calls with one question: "Are our customers OK, and what is this costing us?"

Walk the customer through how the team answers that call, screen by screen. The story, the sites and the faults are the same for every persona. What changes is who the customer is, what they buy and what an outage costs. Pick the persona that matches your audience at the top.
