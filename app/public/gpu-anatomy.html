How GPUs are packaged and bought

Whether you can buy a single GPU depends on its form factor. Data-center GPUs come in four shapes, and the shape decides what a "node" looks like on your floor.

PCIe card

Single GPU: yes

Looks like a large graphics card. Plugs into a slot in any compatible server.

Examples
T4, L4, L40S, H100 PCIe / NVL, RTX PRO
Per server
1 to 8 cards
Power
70 to 600 W per card
Cooling
Air
Typical buyer
Enterprise, inference, edge

SXM on an 8-GPU board

Single GPU: no

GPU modules mounted on NVIDIA's HGX board, joined by NVSwitch. Sold only as a complete server.

Examples
H100 SXM, H200, B200, B300
Per server
Always 8, all the same model
Power
700 to 1,200 W per GPU
Cooling
Air or liquid
Sold by
NVIDIA (DGX), Dell, HPE, Supermicro, Lenovo

Full rack

Single GPU: no

The rack itself is the product: 72 GPUs, switches, power and liquid cooling, delivered as one unit.

Examples
GB200 NVL72, GB300 NVL72
Per rack
72 GPUs in 18 trays of 4
Power
about 120 to 140 kW per rack
Cooling
Liquid only
Typical buyer
Hyperscalers, neoclouds

Cloud rental

Single GPU: yes

Rent by the hour from a cloud or neocloud: a slice of a GPU, one GPU, a full node or a whole pod.

Smallest unit
A MIG slice (1/7 of a GPU)
Common unit
1 GPU, or an 8-GPU node
Billing
Per GPU-hour, or reserved contract
You manage
Software only; no power or cooling
Typical buyer
Startups, ML teams, burst demand
The mixing rule

One GPU model per node. Mix models at the cluster level.

An 8-GPU board only accepts eight identical GPUs. A PCIe server could physically hold two different cards, but they cannot work together on one job and they complicate power, cooling and billing, so operators avoid it.

Instead, nodes with the same GPU are grouped into pools, and the scheduler sends each job to the right pool.

Can I put 4 × T4 and 4 × H100 in the same node?

On an 8-GPU HGX board: no. All eight GPUs must be the same SXM model, and a T4 is a PCIe card that does not fit the board.

In a PCIe server: technically yes, but not done in production. The two cards cannot share one job efficiently (no NVLink between them, very different memory and speed), and power, cooling and billing get harder.

Can I buy a single H100?

Yes as a PCIe card (H100 PCIe or H100 NVL), which goes into a standard server.

No as an SXM module. The top-performance SXM version is sold only inside an 8-GPU server. In the cloud you can rent a single H100 by the hour.

Is a node always 8 GPUs?

No. 8 is the standard for HGX/DGX servers. GB200 compute trays hold 4 GPUs, and PCIe servers hold anywhere from 1 to 8 cards. Store the GPU count per node instead of assuming 8.

What is MIG?

Multi-Instance GPU splits one large GPU (A100, H100, B200) into up to 7 isolated slices, each with its own memory. Clouds use it to rent fractions of a GPU, and enterprises use it to share one GPU between several small inference jobs.

What does this mean for a datacenter operations product?

Model the GPU model and count per node, and report capacity per model ("H100: 80 free, L4: 40 free"), not as one GPU total.

Expect all three shapes on one estate: PCIe servers in enterprise rooms, 8-GPU nodes in AI halls, and NVL72 racks in liquid-cooled pods. Power, cooling and the unit you repair differ for each.
