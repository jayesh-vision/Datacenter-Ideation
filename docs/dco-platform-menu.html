Datacenter screens in the platform menu

Today the whole Datacenter solution is one Data Center card in Plan to Build (plus Datacenter Ops in Ops Toolkit). Below, each Datacenter screen becomes its own card in the section where it belongs, and a card with several working areas lists them as sub-menus (19-Oct-2026): each sub-menu is its own URL, and the screen then shows no tab row of its own (only the tabs merged into that sub-menu). Blue cards are the Datacenter screens (click a title or a sub-menu to open it as the platform would, without our header and menu); grey cards are the platform's existing ones.
