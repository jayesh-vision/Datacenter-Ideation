Questions about SynapseDC.

Have questions? Reach out anytime.

Talk to us
Does SynapseDC replace our DCIM or BMS?

No. It reads from the BMS and the plant with a read-only role and never controls equipment. Its DCIM can be your record of the physical estate, or sit over the one you have.

Is SynapseDC only for operations?

No. It covers planning (capacity, new halls, refreshes), the build (DCIM, projects, commissioning) and operations, on one estate model.

Which business models does SynapseDC cover?

Colocation and GPU cloud (NeoCloud) in the first release; cloud, enterprise and telco datacenters follow on the same estate model.

Can SynapseDC run air-gapped?

Yes. It deploys on-premises, in your cloud or air-gapped. The on-site collector only ever connects outbound.
